01 / About

Creative work, human stories.

After graduating as a soprano into COVID-19, I founded Showcase Creative Studio in 2020. Since then, I've helped platform hundreds of people and organisations in the music industry through design and storytelling.

I write, collaborate across creative disciplines and serve on the board of trustees for Song in the City.

My work is driven by a simple mission: to create space for people who might otherwise feel pushed out, to build community and imagine what comes next.
